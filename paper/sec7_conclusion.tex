\section{Conclusions and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This article presented doubly nested semantic communication, a token-level transceiver with a large-language-model prior that is nested in rate and in time, and charged every part of its operation in channel symbols. The rate-nested codebook with per-shell block power control makes every prefix a valid transmission and token-level incremental redundancy exact, at no measurable cost at the prefix that was checked. A Bayesian beam-search receiver requests further symbols only for tokens it is unsure of, and three receiver memories adapt its prior at three timescales, with every shipped bit recorded.

On a hierarchical drift stream with six seeds and a decoder chosen on validation text, DN-SC reached 90\,\% token accuracy with 7.08 real channel symbols per token against 9.23 for a frozen fixed-rate receiver with the same decoder, a saving of 23.3\,\% with the return-link signaling charged, observed on every seed, and 27.9\,\% (95\,\% CI 21.6--31.7\,\%) when the audited messages were decoded for real. Most of the gain of the memories came from the conversation context, which costs nothing on the air; the shipped memories lowered the prior by a further 0.37 bits per token, but paid for their synchronization, relative to the context memory with refinement, only when one update served more than about 6100 messages. For a single link, the context memory with selective refinement is the economical design; shipped adaptation becomes worthwhile when one update serves a cell or a multicast group.

Future work will (i) repeat the study with larger language models and on streams with naturally occurring drift; (ii) replace the capacity charge of signaling and synchronization by implemented codes on an error-prone return link, with the delay of the rounds modeled; (iii) share one shipped update among the users of a cell, the regime in which the crossover analysis predicts that adaptation pays; and (iv) derive the shipping period of each memory from its size and drift rate, so that the schedule of the memories becomes a consequence of the cost model rather than a design choice.
