\section*{Data Availability Statement}
{\sloppy
The code, the configurations, the decoder selection on validation text, the pre-specified protocol and every result file behind the tables and figures of this article, including the per-step logs of all stream runs, are openly available at \url{https://github.com/abraheemrashid99-sys/Wiley_paper} (branch \texttt{review-fixes}).
